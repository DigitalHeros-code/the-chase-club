How it works

Three steps between you and your first run with the club.

Step 1

Fill the join form

Tell us your pace, your usual distance, and which mornings work. Takes about a minute.

Step 2

Get added to the crew chat

We'll drop you into the WhatsApp group where routes, meet points, and changes get shared.

Step 3

Show up and run

Turn up at the meet point. Someone will run your pace — that's the only rule we enforce.
